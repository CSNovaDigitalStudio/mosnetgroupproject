ABOUT THE COMPANY

A diversified company with interests across multiple ventures.

MOSNET GROUP (Pty) Ltd is a South African company involved in different business ventures. The company is structured to pursue opportunities across more than one area of business, with each venture contributing to its broader growth and development.

Construction is one of the company’s featured areas of operation. Through MOSNET GROUP Construction Services, the company undertakes building, bricklaying, roofing, waterproofing, painting, plastering, ceiling, tiling and plumbing projects. The company is based in Pretoria and considers suitable projects across South Africa.
